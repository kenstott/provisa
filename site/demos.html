Provisa / Live demos

Live demos

Each demo opens in your browser with its own data already loaded, the example queries to run, and the tour. No account, nothing to install.

Instant data lake

A city's open-data drop -- 311 complaints in monthly CSV files, building permits in Parquet, the budget in Excel and inspections in JSON -- read in place as one governed catalog, with no load step: which neighborhoods have the most complaints near active construction?

Open live demo

Insider review

A bank's outbound mail matched against its control room's watch list of deals in discussion -- by name, ticker, code name and, through a local embedding model, by meaning -- and checked for customer data: one ranked review queue, with the watch list itself never shown to the reviewer.

Open live demo

Data you can trust

A chain of shops' daily sales feed, landed into bronze and refined into silver and gold by materialized views that check every batch: landing a batch is all it takes, a clean batch reaches gold, and a bad one is shown failed and never reaches silver.

Open live demo

Product reviews

An electronics shop's catalog in MongoDB, its customers' reviews in Elasticsearch and who bought what in Neo4j, joined on the product: which products do reviews say the battery dies on, and what else did their buyers buy?

Open live demo

Shakespeare's sonnets

Shakespeare's 154 sonnets in Postgres, embedded into Qdrant, searched by meaning and grown into a graph: the themes that run through them, and the lines where Shakespeare echoes himself.

Open live demo

Test data

A shop's production database, read in prod as it is and in two Test (fake) environments, dev and test, as realistic generated data computed by the engine at the read. Can I test against realistic data without touching production? The same queries, views and joins answer in all three; only prod sees the real rows.

Open live demo

Web shop logs

A web shop's nginx access log and its database's statement log, read as tables and joined to the shop's own data: the slowest pages and the SQL behind them, the requests that failed in the database, the customers who were served an error, and the products viewed but never bought.

Open live demo
