Live demos

Each demo opens in your browser with its own data already loaded, the example queries to run, and the tour. No account, nothing to install.

Weather and crop insurance losses

A crop insurer's portfolio of Corn Belt farms, in its own database, joined by county and crop year to the USDA's crop insurance losses by cause, NOAA's daily weather and NASS's county yields from government data: does weather explain the losses in our portfolio?

Predict customer churn

Which customers are about to cancel? A subscription business's customers and their usage, read into pandas in the browser as the analyst role, email addresses masked, and a scikit-learn model trained on them in the Notebook tab, from a notebook the demo ships.

Data vault

A data vault declared over two live systems from Northwind, an order system and a CRM: hubs, links and satellites as views the engine builds and refreshes as new rows arrive, personal data masked in the satellites, and one point-in-time query that answers what was known about a customer, from which source, and when.

Instant data lake

A city's open-data drop -- 311 complaints in monthly CSV files, building permits in Parquet, the budget in Excel and inspections in JSON -- read in place as one governed catalog, with no load step: which neighborhoods have the most complaints near active construction?

One model, app and BI

A retailer's order-history screen and its BI dashboard read one model of PostgreSQL, MongoDB and an internal REST service. The app asks one nested GraphQL query as the signed-in customer; Apache Superset charts revenue and late shipments over the PostgreSQL wire protocol as the analyst.

Live order stream

A coffee shop's orders arrive live on a Kafka topic and are joined to its catalog in Postgres. Which regions placed the most orders in the last five minutes? A view refreshed by every batch of orders that lands answers it in SQL, GraphQL and REST, and a subscription watches it move.

Test data

A shop's production database, read in prod as it is and in two Test (fake) environments, dev and test, as realistic generated data computed by the engine at the read. Can I test against realistic data without touching production? The same queries, views and joins answer in all three; only prod sees the real rows.
