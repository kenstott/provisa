Promo Video Concepts

Working concepts for Provisa's promo videos: the main cut, the shot plan it is moving toward, and the tagline each demo's video will carry.

Download the MP4 (16:9).

Shot plan

Four statements, one idea each, held long enough to read. No full-screen app shots: each shot starts already cropped to the part that matters, with at most one slow camera move.

StatementOn screenTime
Connect anything.The source picker, already zoomed onto a readable slice of the list, drifting slowly and ending on Parquet. No lingering once it lands.8–10 s
Federate it.Polly's answer, large: the relationship it created between two tables. If the text reads too small, a simple line between two labeled boxes.8 s
Query it nine ways.One result table, large, with a wheel that spins through the nine surfaces at about 2 seconds each: GraphQL, SQL, Cypher, gRPC, OpenAPI, JSON:API, Python, Notebook, natural language.~18 s
Provisa. It Really Is That EasyEnd card.4 s

Security is a separate idea. It gets its own short video or appears in the per-demo videos.
