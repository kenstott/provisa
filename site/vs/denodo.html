Provisa / Works with / Provisa and Denodo

Keep Denodo. Extend it past SQL.

Denodo is a mature virtualization platform with two decades of enterprise connectors and a support organization behind it. Provisa shares its premise of governed access over distributed data without duplicating it, and works with it: Provisa registers Denodo's views as governed tables, and SQL keeps running through Denodo's optimizer.

What Denodo keeps doing

  • SQL virtualization over your sources, with its commercial optimizer and broad connector library
  • REST, OData and GraphQL published over virtual SQL views
  • Enterprise on-premises installs and its regulated-industry track record

What Provisa adds

  • GraphQL, SQL and Cypher as peers over one model
  • An MCP server where the OAuth token maps to a role, so agents inherit the same six access-control layers as any person
  • Publishing to external catalogs and importing from your semantic layer
  • A single wheel for a developer machine, with no Docker, no JVM and no root

Use them together

If Denodo already handles SQL virtualization over your sources, Provisa connects over JDBC and registers those views as governed tables. SQL requests pass through Denodo's optimizer as they always have.

What changes is that the same model now answers in GraphQL and Cypher too, enforces the same rules on an MCP connection, and publishes metadata to the catalog tools you may already run. You can extend it step by step and keep what works.

If you only need Denodo

Your evaluation weighs connector breadth, optimizer maturity, and an enterprise support organization with decades of regulated-industry track record. Your primary consumers speak SQL, REST, or OData. Denodo is the strong choice on its own for that profile.
